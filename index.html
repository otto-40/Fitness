Standing Orders
  • Progressive overload: add reps to top of range, then add load. Matching last week in a deficit still counts.
  • Pulling lifts: only progress load after 1–2 quiet-elbow weeks. The tendon sets the pace.
  • Skip: pull-ups, direct curls, heavy grip work — until elbow resolves.
  • Zone 2 = nasal-breathing pace, can hold a conversation.
  • Fat loss engine: 300–500 kcal/day deficit, protein 1.6–2.2 g/kg.
