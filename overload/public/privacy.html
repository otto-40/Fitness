Privacy policy

Overload · last updated 27 September 2026

The short version

Overload does not collect, send or sell any of your data. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you log stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

Your workouts, routines, exercises, body measurements and settings are saved on your device only: in the iPhone app's own storage, or in your browser's storage on the web version. The developer cannot see them.

Notifications

Rest-over alerts are scheduled on your device by your device. Nothing is sent to a server to deliver them. You can turn them off at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Backups

Export creates a file that you choose where to save or send, using your device's share sheet. Import reads a file you pick. Nothing leaves your device unless you send it yourself.

Deleting your data

Settings → Danger zone → Erase all data removes everything. Deleting the app removes everything too.

Children

Overload collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/otto-40/Fitness.
