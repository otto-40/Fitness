## Promotional text (170)
Your rest counts down on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island, with exactly what's next: the exercise, the set, the weight and the reps.

## Description (4000)
Overload is a strength training log built for the gym floor: one hand, big buttons and nothing in the way.

LOG A SET WITH ONE THUMB
The next set is always at the bottom of the screen: the exercise, set 2 of 4, last time's numbers, and big +/− buttons for weight and reps. Tap Log set and the rest timer starts.

KNOW WHAT'S NEXT
While you rest, Overload shows the countdown and your next set. Lock your phone and the countdown carries on on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island, and a notification tells you when rest is over, with the exercise, weight and reps.

PROGRESSIVE OVERLOAD, MADE VISIBLE
• Estimated 1RM, heaviest weight, best volume and rep records for every exercise
• Weekly volume, sessions, muscle balance and aerobic minutes against the 150-minute guideline
• Rate each set Easy, Moderate or Hard. When everything felt easy, Overload suggests adding weight.

PLANS THAT FIT YOUR WEEK
Answer a few questions and get a starting plan for your schedule, goal and equipment. You can also build your own routines, with supersets, rest times and rep targets. Start a session in one tap, or repeat any past workout.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
No account. No cloud. No ads, no tracking. Everything lives on your iPhone, and you can export a backup whenever you like.

Also included: warm-up, drop and failure sets, a plate-step keypad, body measurements with trends, a workout calendar, a streak, light and dark themes, and kilograms or pounds.
